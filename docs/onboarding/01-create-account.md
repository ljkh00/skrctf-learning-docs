# 1. Create your account

**Goal:** an activated account and a team, ready to play. **Time:** 5–10 minutes.

!!! info "Solo or team, everyone follows the same steps"
    SKRCTF scores by team. Even if you play alone, you must create a team.

1. **Register.** Go to [skrctf.me](https://skrctf.me) and select **Register**. Sign up with your name and email.
2. **Activate your account.** Open the email from SKRCTF and click the activation link. (check spam/junk folder)
3. **Create a team.** Log in and create a team, even for solo players.
4. **Share your team password** (only if teammates are joining). They use it to join your team. Keep this credentials for `webshell` later.

!!! warning "Common mistakes"
    - No activation email? Check your spam folder.
    - Skipping team creation: you cannot submit flags until you have a team.

Full visual guide on the platform: [skrctf.me/guide](https://skrctf.me/guide)

**Done when:** you are logged in and can see your team on the **Teams** page.
