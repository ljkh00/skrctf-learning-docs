# Start here

SKRCTF is a free platform where you learn cybersecurity by solving hands-on challenges. Each solved challenge gives you a **flag**, a secret text you submit for points.

## Your first 30 minutes
1. [Create your account](onboarding/01-create-account.md) — 5 min
2. [Solve your first challenge](onboarding/02-first-challenge.md) — 15 min
3. [Learn the webshell](onboarding/03-webshell.md) — 10 min

Then follow the [Beginner pathway](pathways/beginner.md).

!!! tip "Stuck?"
    Being stuck is normal. Players who rated challenges "very challenging" made the most attempts, and kept going. See [What the data says](insights/metrics.md).
